# Oxygen Delivery Evidence

**Living Scientific Repository**

Evidence, context and analysis across human medicine, veterinary medicine, transplantation and oxygen biology.

## Purpose

Oxygen Delivery Evidence is a broader scientific evidence layer for research on oxygen delivery and its limitations. It is intentionally separate from repositories focused primarily on direct BHOC / HBOC evidence.

The repository is designed to organize evidence on:

- transfusion and donor blood
- tissue oxygenation
- hypoxia and ischemia
- microcirculation
- mitochondria
- nitric oxide biology
- oxygen sensing and HIF
- hemolysis and free heme
- organ perfusion
- blood storage
- compatibility and alloimmunization
- shock and resuscitation
- comparative oxygen-delivery strategies

## Published Evidence, Analysis and Perspective

The repository deliberately separates three levels:

**Published Evidence**: findings reported by the original source.

**Derived Analysis**: calculations or structured comparisons made from published values.

**Scientific Perspective**: interpretation of the combined evidence and research implications.

Research hypotheses are labeled separately and are not presented as established facts.

## Copyright handling

This public repository stores metadata, identifiers, structured summaries, original analyses and links to scientific or regulatory sources. It does not republish copyrighted journal articles unless reuse is explicitly permitted.

Public availability does not make the original compilation, taxonomy, analytical framework, derived analyses or editorial structure open source or open data.
