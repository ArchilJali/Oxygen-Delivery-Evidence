# Oxygen Delivery Evidence Taxonomy

## Purpose

The taxonomy separates direct HBOC evidence from the broader science of oxygen delivery while allowing the same publication to appear in multiple evidence views without duplication.

## Evidence roles

### direct
The intervention or mechanism under study directly concerns HBOC, cell-free hemoglobin or an oxygen carrier.

### comparator
The source characterizes the current standard or alternative against which an oxygen-delivery strategy is assessed, such as donor RBC transfusion, fluids or conventional blood-bank logistics.

### foundation
The source explains underlying oxygen biology, physiology, molecular mechanisms or disease processes relevant to oxygen delivery.

### context
The source provides epidemiology, logistics, blood availability, health-system context or other background that does not directly test the intervention.

### regulatory
Primary regulator material such as FDA or EMA approval, labeling, safety or efficacy records.

### guideline
Professional consensus statement or clinical guideline.

### review
Systematic, scoping or narrative review.

## Relation to BHOC

- direct: directly studies a BHOC/HBOC-relevant carrier, platform or predecessor
- relevant: not direct BHOC evidence, but informs a comparator, endpoint, risk, mechanism or development question
- contextual: broader background that helps explain the clinical or biological problem
- none: no material BHOC relationship, but may remain relevant to the broader oxygen-delivery repository

## Analysis labels

### Published Evidence
What the source itself reports.

### Derived Analysis
A calculation or structured comparison performed from source values. The source values and calculation must be reproducible.

### Scientific Perspective
An interpretation of the combined evidence and what it may mean for research or development.

### Research Hypothesis
A testable question that is not yet established by the evidence.

## Rule against duplication

A scientific publication is stored once as one master record. Domain pages, topic pages, comparator views and BHOC-related views reference the same master record through metadata.

## Search priority

Search should support combinations of:
- domain
- species
- topic
- intervention
- comparator
- endpoint
- study design
- evidence role
- relation to BHOC
- year
- author
- DOI / PMID / PMCID

Examples:
- dog + transfusion + survival
- cat + compatible blood + availability
- HBOC + RBC + tissue PO2
- transplant + ischemia + mitochondria
- human + donor blood + shortage
- veterinary + alloimmunization + crossmatch
