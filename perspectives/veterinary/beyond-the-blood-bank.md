## Central question

Blood transfusion saves lives. The question is whether the donor-blood system, with its biological and operational constraints, should remain the only architecture veterinary medicine relies on for restoring oxygen delivery.

The final physiological objective is not simply to increase PCV, hematocrit or hemoglobin concentration. The objective is effective oxygen delivery to tissues.

## Core evidence to develop in the full article

- Conventional transfusion outcomes: Callan 1996, Hann 2014, Radulescu 2021, Hall 2024.
- Alloimmunization: Goy-Thollot 2017, Herter 2022.
- Oxyglobin pivotal regulatory evidence: FDA NADA 141-067.
- Direct Oxyglobin vs pRBC comparison: Zambelli and Leisewitz 2009.
- Tissue oxygenation: Standl 1996 and 2003.
- Repeat-dose immunology: Hamilton 2001.
- Feline operational evidence: Gibson 2002, Weingart and Kohn 2008.

## Working conclusion

We cannot build a complete donor-blood system for every species on Earth.

But we can investigate a new layer of oxygen-delivery readiness for all of them.
